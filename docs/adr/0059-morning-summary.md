# 0059 — Morning summary

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-19
- Builds on: ADR 0021 (Inbox), ADR 0037 (PR watcher), ADR 0041 (CI watcher), ADR 0020 (notifications)

## Context

Spec §7.4 asks for a scheduled nightly triage that ends in a morning summary with a link to the Inbox. Sync, triage, the PR watcher and the CI watcher already run on a poll; nothing gathered them for the user.

## Decision

1. **A summary is derived, not generated by a model.** `SummaryService` (`apps/daemon/src/summary/`) reads the Inbox, open CI failures, Navarch's open PRs, pending approvals and finished runs, and records `summary.generated` with the whole summary as its payload. No model call, no side effect besides the event.
2. **Schedule.** With `summary.enabled` (default `false`), a one-minute tick makes the summary when the local time has passed `summary.at` (`08:00`) and none was made today. It first syncs every project, the PR watcher and the CI watcher; a failed refresh does not stop the summary. `since` is the previous summary's time, or 24 h before.
3. **Content.** New or changed open items (at most 10 listed, `start_run` suggestions first, then by complexity), possible duplicates, open CI failures, Navarch PRs open longer than `summary.stalled_days` (3), approvals waiting, runs finished since and how many failed, billed cost, and suggested runs (`start_run`, complexity ≤ 3) with the sum of their Inbox estimates.
4. **Delivery.** The Notifier turns the event into a push "Morning summary" linking to `/home`, with counts only; titles stay out of the push. Quiet hours apply. `GET /api/v1/summary` (devices may read it), `POST /api/v1/summary` (desktop only, syncs first) and `navarch summary [--now]`.

## Consequences

- 🟡 Triage runs after a sync and may not have finished when the summary is built; such items show as not triaged and appear triaged in the next one.
- 🟡 The summary is a snapshot: items handled later are not removed from it.
- Home shows the latest summary in a card (`SummaryCard.vue`, store `useSummaryStore`, updated on `summary.generated`); "Make one now" is desktop only.
- 🔴 No per-project filter, no weekday schedule.
